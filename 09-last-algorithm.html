Last algorithm — the final turn

Everything is in place except the four top edges. One more algorithm cycles them round and the cube is solved.

Goal: the whole cube solved. After this step there is nothing left to do.

The algorithm from the document

Front twice, top, left, right reverse, front twice, left reverse, right, top, front twice.

This one cycles three top edges around: the edge at the front goes to the left, the left edge goes to the right, and the right edge comes to the front. The corners stay put.

How to use it

  1. Keep the yellow face on top.
  2. Find a top edge that is already in its correct place (its two colours match the two centres it touches).
  3. Hold the cube so that correct edge is at the back.
  4. Run the algorithm and look at the top again.
  5. If the edges are still not all home, turn the cube so a correct edge is at the back and run it once more.

Solved!

Top face — all yellow, everything lined up

White cross and corners below, all four sides matching their centres, yellow on top. Well done.

If the edges will not line up after two runs, do the algorithm once more from a different holding — you may have started with no edge in the right place. The cycle always lands the cube on solved within a couple of runs.
Reading the sheet. The last two algorithms come from a handwritten sheet and were the hardest to read. Both were checked on a cube model: this one cycles three top edges and keeps everything else intact.
