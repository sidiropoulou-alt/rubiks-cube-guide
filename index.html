Know your cube: the centres and the moves

Before you turn anything, learn the six centres and the six moves. Everything else in this guide is written in these moves.

Where the centres are

A cube has 6 faces. In the middle of every face there is a centre — one square, and it never leaves its face, no matter how you turn the cube. That is why the centre tells you what colour the whole face must become.

The cube unfolded. Each centre is the middle square of its face.
  • Top (T) — the face pointing up. In this guide we hold the cube with the yellow centre on top when we build the daisy.
  • Bottom (D) — the face pointing down.
  • Front (F) — the face looking at you.
  • Back (B) — the face away from you.
  • Right (R) — the face on your right hand.
  • Left (L) — the face on your left hand.

Tip

Hold the cube the same way every time: front towards you, top up. If you turn the whole cube, “front” and “right” turn with it.

All the moves

Each move turns one face a quarter turn (90°). A letter on its own means turn that face clockwise — clockwise as you look straight at that face. A small 1 (or an apostrophe, ') means turn it the other way. A 2 — or the letter written twice — means turn it twice.
