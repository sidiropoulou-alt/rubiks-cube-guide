Step 2 · Page 3 of 9

Align the centres and make the white cross

Take the daisy and fold each white petal down to the bottom, so the white cross appears — with every side colour matching its centre.

Goal: a white cross on the bottom face, where each white edge's other colour sits directly under the centre of the same colour.

What it looks like

Bottom face — white cross
Front face — white edge under the green centre

The move

  1. Keep the yellow centre on top and the white petals around it.
  2. Pick one petal. Look at its side colour.
  3. Turn the top (T or T1) until that side colour is above the centre of the same colour. Now the petal and the centre line up.
  4. Turn that face twice — F F, R R, B B or L L — and the white sticker drops to the bottom.
  5. Do the same for the other three petals.

Example: line the petal up, then turn the front twice.

Why the centre matters here. The centres never move, so “match the petal to the centre” is really “put the edge where it belongs”. If you skip this, the cross will be white but the side colours will be in the wrong places.
Check: with the cube white-side-down, look at each side face. Its bottom edge should be white on the bottom and the side colour should join the centre above it.
