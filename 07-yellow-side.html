Step 6 · Page 7 of 9 · Document algorithm

Full yellow side

The cross is there; now the four corners get their yellow stickers facing up, until the whole top face is yellow.

Goal: the entire top face is yellow. The sides may still look scrambled — that is fine, we fix them on the last two pages.

The algorithm from the document

Right, top, right reverse, top, right, top twice, right reverse.

This is a very well-known little algorithm (cubers call it the Sune). It twists the yellow corners without spoiling the cross.

How to use it

  1. Hold the cube with the yellow cross on top.
  2. Look at the top face. Find the corner that already shows yellow on top, and turn the top (T) so that corner sits at the front-left (the top-left of the front face).
  3. Run the algorithm once and look again.
  4. Still not all yellow? Turn the top so a corner that is not yet yellow sits in that same front-left spot, and run it again.
  5. Repeat until all four corners show yellow on top.

Finished top face

Top face — all yellow

It may take two or three runs, and the top may need re-turning between runs.

Don't panic if the cube looks messy on the sides while you do this — the Sune always puts the first two layers back when the sequence is finished. Do the whole algorithm, then look.
