The Daisy

Four white edges around the yellow centre. That flower shape is called the daisy, and it is the easiest way to start.

Goal: hold the cube with the yellow centre on top, and bring the four white edges up next to it. The top then looks like a flower: yellow middle, four white petals.

What it looks like

Top face — yellow centre, white petals

How to build it

  1. Turn the cube so the yellow centre is on top.
  2. Look for a white edge (a piece with a white sticker and one other colour).
  3. If it is in the bottom layer, turn that side face twice (R R, F F, L L or B B) to bring the white sticker up to the top.
  4. If it is already in the top layer but not next to the yellow centre, turn the top (T) until it is free, then fix the piece you need.
  5. Repeat until all four white edges sit around the yellow centre.
Don't worry about the sides yet. On the daisy the petals' side colours can point anywhere. You fix that in the next step. Right now only two things matter: yellow centre up, four white edges around it.
Watch out: if a white edge is already in the top layer, turning the top (T) moves it around the yellow centre without breaking the petals you already placed.
