The Fish / Cross

Now we work on the top. Hold the cube with the yellow centre up and use one algorithm to grow a yellow cross out of the yellow shapes you can see.

Goal: a yellow cross on the top face — the yellow centre plus all four yellow edges, forming a plus sign. The corners do not matter yet.

The algorithm from the document

Front, top, right, top reverse, right reverse, front reverse.

Keep the yellow centre on top. Look at how many yellow edge stickers are showing, then hold the cube as shown and run the algorithm:

The shapes, in order

Dot — any holding
L-shape — hold it in the top-left
Line — hold it across
Cross — done!

Dot → L → line → cross. If you start on a dot, you may need the algorithm up to three times, checking the shape and the holding each time.

Which shape do I have? Count the yellow stickers on the top edges: none = dot, two together in a corner = L, two in a straight line = line, four = cross. The centre is always yellow, so it does not count.
Fish. As the yellow face fills in you will meet a shape that looks like a fish — the document names this step “Fish / Cross” because of it. Keep the yellow centre up; the next page finishes the yellow side.
