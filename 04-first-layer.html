Step 3 · Page 4 of 9

Complete the first layer

Now the four white corners go in, using one short algorithm — from the right or from the left, depending on where the corner's slot is.

Goal: the whole bottom face is white, and the bottom row of every side face matches its centre. That is the whole first layer finished.

Hold the cube like this

White centre down, yellow centre up. The white cross you just made stays on the bottom the whole time.

  1. Find a white corner in the top layer (a piece with a white sticker and two other colours).
  2. Turn the top (T) so that corner sits directly above the slot where it belongs — the corner whose two side colours match the two centres it touches.
  3. Look at which side the corner needs to go down:

Right side → R T1 R1

Right, top reverse, right reverse.

Left side → L T L1

Left, top, left reverse.

Repeat the right algorithm (or the left one) until the corner is sitting in its slot with white facing down. Then do the next corner, and the next, until all four are in.

Finished first layer

Bottom face — all white
Front face — bottom row solved
Good to know. You may need to run the algorithm two, three or even five times for one corner. That is normal — keep the same corner above its slot and keep going. If a white corner is stuck in the bottom layer but in the wrong place, do the algorithm once to send it up, then place it properly.
